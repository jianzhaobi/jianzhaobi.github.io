## National posture

- **US:** The August 22 IMSR kept the United States at National Preparedness Level 5, with 78 uncontained large fires, 125 active incidents, and 22,751 personnel assigned. (Preparedness Level 5: national resources are fully committed, active geographic areas may need emergency measures, and potential for additional significant fires is high) ([NIFC Incident Management Situation Report — August 22, 2026](https://www.nifc.gov/nicc/sitreprt.pdf))

It showed 105 full-response fires covering 611,399.0 hectares, 3,302 out-of-control fires covering 151,719.6 hectares, and 4,860 fires covering 4,276,685.4 hectares year to date. (Preparedness Level 5: national demand is extreme, resources are fully committed, and international resources are being mobilized to sustain operations) ([CIFFC National Fire Situation Report — August 22, 2026](https://ciffc.net/situation/2026-08-22); [CIFFC Current Fires Summary — August 22, 2026](https://ciffc.net/situation/2026-08-22))
