# U.S.–Canada Wildfire Structural-Impact Briefing — 2026-08-14

**Cutoff:** August 14, 2026 at 10:00 PM EDT

## National posture

- **US:** The August 14 IMSR placed the United States at National Preparedness Level 5, with 93 uncontained large fires, 152 active incidents, and 29,612 personnel assigned. (Preparedness Level 5: national resources are fully committed, active geographic areas may need emergency measures, and potential for additional significant fires is high) ([NIFC Incident Management Situation Report — August 14, 2026](https://www.nifc.gov/nicc/sitreprt.pdf))
- **CA:** The Aug 14 CIFFC situation report kept Canada at National Preparedness Level 5. It showed 110 full-response fires covering 999,324.7 hectares, 3,293 out-of-control fires covering 139,671.5 hectares, and 4,702 fires covering 4,109,604.3 hectares year to date. (Preparedness Level 5: national demand is extreme, resources are fully committed, and international resources are being mobilized to sustain operations) ([CIFFC National Fire Situation Report — August 14, 2026](https://ciffc.net/situation/2026-08-14); [CIFFC Current Fires Summary — August 14, 2026](https://ciffc.net/situation/2026-08-14))
