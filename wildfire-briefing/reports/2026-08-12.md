# U.S.–Canada Wildfire Structural-Impact Briefing — 2026-08-12

**Cutoff:** August 12, 2026 at 10:00 PM EDT

## National posture

- **US:** The August 12 IMSR placed the United States at National Preparedness Level 5, with 101 uncontained large fires, 156 active incidents, and 30,189 personnel assigned. (Preparedness Level 5: national resources are fully committed, active geographic areas may need emergency measures, and potential for additional significant fires is high) ([NIFC Incident Management Situation Report — August 12, 2026](https://www.nifc.gov/nicc/sitreprt.pdf))

It showed 116 full-response fires covering 991,719.8 hectares, 3,255 out-of-control fires covering 126,171.1 hectares, and 4,654 fires covering 4,082,236.6 hectares year to date. (Preparedness Level 5: national demand is extreme, resources are fully committed, and international resources are being mobilized to sustain operations) ([CIFFC National Fire Situation Report — August 12, 2026](https://ciffc.net/situation/2026-08-12); [CIFFC Current Fires Summary — August 12, 2026](https://ciffc.net/situation/2026-08-12))
