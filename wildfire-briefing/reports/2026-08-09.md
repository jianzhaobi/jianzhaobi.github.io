## National posture

- **US:** The August 9 IMSR placed the United States at National Preparedness Level 5, with 94 uncontained large fires, 147 active incidents, and 30,419 personnel assigned. (Preparedness Level 5: national resources are fully committed, active geographic areas may need emergency measures, and potential for additional significant fires is high) ([NIFC Incident Management Situation Report — August 9, 2026](https://www.nifc.gov/nicc/sitreprt.pdf))

The Aug 8 current-fires summary showed 123 full-response fires covering 988,546.8 hectares and 4,566 fires covering 3,902,318.5 hectares year to date; the Aug 9 sitrep was timestamped 8:01 PM EDT, just after cutoff. (Preparedness Level 5: national demand is extreme, resources are fully committed, and international resources are being mobilized to sustain operations) ([CIFFC National Fire Situation Report — August 8, 2026](https://ciffc.net/situation/); [CIFFC Current Fires Summary — August 8, 2026](https://ciffc.net/summary))
