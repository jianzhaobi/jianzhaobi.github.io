## National posture

- **US:** The August 20 IMSR kept the United States at National Preparedness Level 5, with 72 uncontained large fires, 127 active incidents, and 25,248 personnel assigned. (Preparedness Level 5: national resources are fully committed, active geographic areas may need emergency measures, and potential for additional significant fires is high) ([NIFC Incident Management Situation Report — August 20, 2026](https://www.nifc.gov/nicc/sitreprt.pdf))

It showed 101 full-response fires covering 607,603.1 hectares, 3,284 out-of-control fires covering 149,121.4 hectares, and 4,823 fires covering 4,127,530.9 hectares year to date. (Preparedness Level 5: national demand is extreme, resources are fully committed, and international resources are being mobilized to sustain operations) ([CIFFC National Fire Situation Report — August 20, 2026](https://ciffc.net/situation/2026-08-20); [CIFFC Current Fires Summary — August 20, 2026](https://ciffc.net/situation/2026-08-20))
