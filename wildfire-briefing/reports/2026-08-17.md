# U.S.–Canada Wildfire Structural-Impact Briefing — 2026-08-17

**Cutoff:** August 17, 2026 at 10:01 PM EDT

## National posture

- **US:** The August 17 IMSR placed the United States at National Preparedness Level 5, with 80 uncontained large fires, 144 active incidents, and 28,710 personnel assigned. (Preparedness Level 5: national resources are fully committed, active geographic areas may need emergency measures, and potential for additional significant fires is high) ([NIFC Incident Management Situation Report — August 17, 2026](https://www.nifc.gov/nicc/sitreprt.pdf))
- **CA:** The Aug 17 CIFFC situation report kept Canada at National Preparedness Level 5. It showed 105 full-response fires covering 605,244.4 hectares, 3,234 out-of-control fires covering 148,976.7 hectares, and 4,779 fires covering 4,114,037.9 hectares year to date. (Preparedness Level 5: national demand is extreme, resources are fully committed, and international resources are being mobilized to sustain operations) ([CIFFC National Fire Situation Report — August 17, 2026](https://ciffc.net/situation/2026-08-17); [CIFFC Current Fires Summary — August 17, 2026](https://ciffc.net/situation/2026-08-17))
